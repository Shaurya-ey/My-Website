01 About

Turning curiosity into code

I'm Shaurya, an engineering student from India studying Computer Science with a specialisation in AI/ML. I got into programming a couple of years ago, and somewhere along the way I got completely hooked on the intersection of software and hardware — training a model, then putting it on a microcontroller, wiring up a PCB, that sort of thing.

Right now I spend most of my time on computer vision, embedded telemetry and building small tools that make my desk smarter. When I'm not building, I'm usually grinding LeetCode, taking electronics apart to see how they work, or reading about how things fly.

I'm not just learning to code — I'm learning how to think, solve and create.
